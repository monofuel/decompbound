2026-07-25 update: same failure shape during the Dungeon Man "yellow
submarine" cutscene — audio glitched out AND the game soft-locked at the
same moment; emulator restart cleared both (save intact).

Pattern across all three incidents: the lock hits at a **music-change
moment** (inn rest, hotel entry, cutscene), an in-process save reload does
NOT clear it, a full process restart DOES. That rules out battery-save
corruption and points at accumulated emulated-APU state: those moments are
exactly where the main CPU handshakes with the sound driver over ports
$2140-$2143 and spin-waits for an ack — a wedged/desynced SPC700 never
acks (soft lock) while the DSP keeps free-running stale voice state
(glitched audio). Same failure family as the fixed inn-sleep derail
(docs/play-regressions.md §D); the catch-up fix there is capped at 512
polls/frame, which may not cover every wait site. An "APU-HANG?"
diagnostic is being added to snesbus so the next live occurrence
self-reports the stuck port + SPC state; full analysis with ranked
candidates: 2026-07-25 investigation (Bug B) — hang detector first, then
fix + TAS referee test like test_apu_handshake_derail.

2026-07-26/27 RESOLUTION — the Tenda crash series root-caused the family.

Three Tenda Village crashes in one evening (inn entry, Tenda dialogue,
then an 80s session) gave F12-at-hang state captures and finally a
DETERMINISTIC repro, closing the case in three layers:

1. **Crash 1 (BRK sink, NMI masked):** the $214x APU catch-up cap (512
   ticks/frame, the old inn-sleep fix) STARVES the SPC during the KB-scale
   uploadApuPackages transfer ($C0AB06, NMI masked for the IPL stream) →
   stale echo byte → control-flow derail into the unused-vector BRK sink
   $00:5FFF. Fixed: catch-up exemption (16384) while PC is inside the
   handshake routines (upload + waitApuIdleClearSong + readApuPort0).
   Referee: test_apu_upload_starvation.

2. **Crashes 2+3 (WRAM corruption → DMA storm):** a genuine **EarthBound
   ROM hazard** — the CGRAM palette staging buffer ($7E:0240-$043F,
   16 rows × 32 bytes, writer $C473D0 via JSL $C4746B on fade ticks)
   OVERLAPS the CHR-DMA job ring head ($7E:0400-$04FF, slots 0-7). When a
   real CHR job is pending at ring index $00-$3F and a palette rebuild
   fires first, the drain at $C0823C executes palette words as a DMA job —
   decoding to a reverse (B→A) MDMA with a ~48KB size that walks the
   bank-$0E low-WRAM mirror, zeroes the $0020 NMI vector (and NMITIMEN at
   $4200) → JMP ($0020) → BRK storm (the "WRAM fill" = BRK stack frames as
   S wraps). Real hardware would crash the same way given the same
   interleaving; our line-224 NMI model makes the bad ordering more likely
   than cycle-accurate timing, but the alias is in the ROM. Fixed
   (safety rail): runDma blocks B→A MDMA into WRAM mirrors — EB's
   legitimate CHR queue is A→B only. Referee: test_crash3_repro (replays
   the real crash tape; fail-before/pass-after), test_dma_wram_toa_guard.

3. **Detectors** now narrate any recurrence in bin/play_log.txt:
   CPU-DERAIL?, NMI-MASK-STUCK?, DMA-STORM?, WRAM-SPRAY?,
   NMI-QUEUE-STUCK?, NMI-VEC-WIPE?, DMA-WRAM-TOA?, APU-HANG?.

Full forensics: probe_tenda_*.nim, probe_7c_*.nim, probe_crash3*.nim,
probe_queue_poison*.nim under src/probes/. The Scaraba hotel + Dungeon
Man locks were almost certainly layer 1 or 2 of the same family (no
capture survived to distinguish); if either recurs the detectors will say
